Dinner · Fine Dining

Dinner on the bosphorus

A three-hour evening, a multi-course menu — selected Turkish dishes served as the Bosphorus lights up.

What's on the menu

Four courses, served slowly

A multi-course Turkish menu built around classic meze, a warm pastry, a generous main, and dessert. Standard and VIP follow the same arc — VIP differs in meze breadth and adds premium beef to the main course.

01 Meze Standard: 10 varieties · VIP: 15+ varieties

Cold Turkish appetisers served family-style at your table: hummus, ezme (spicy tomato), haydari (strained yoghurt with herbs), fava (broad bean), atom (yoghurt with chili butter), kısır (bulgur salad), yaprak sarması (stuffed vine leaves), çoban salatası, smoked aubergine, beetroot tarator. VIP extends the spread with seasonal specials, octopus and a wider seafood selection.

02 Warm starter Same in both packages

Paçanga böreği — a crisp, fried filo pastry filled with beef pastırma (air-dried cured beef) and melted kashar cheese. A signature Turkish opener served hot, between meze and main.

03 Main course Choose one · seasonal salad included

Each guest picks one main; partners can mix categories. Standard offers four choices below; VIP adds premium beef (tenderloin / ribeye) on top of the same four.

Meat

Grilled Meatballs

Hand-pressed beef and lamb köfte seasoned with cumin, parsley and onion. Served with smoked aubergine purée and grilled vegetables.

Meat

Beef Tenderloin · Ribeye

Cut to order from premium Turkish beef, grilled to your preferred doneness and rested. Served with butter-roasted potatoes and a peppercorn jus.

Chicken

Grilled Chicken Breast

Marinated overnight in olive oil, lemon and herbs, then grilled to order. Served with seasonal roasted vegetables and yoghurt sauce.

Fish

Salmon · Sea Bream

Pan-seared catch of the day with olive oil, lemon and Mediterranean herbs. Served with garlic potatoes and grilled greens.

Vegetarian

Falafel Platter

Crispy chickpea falafel with bulgur pilaf, sautéed seasonal vegetables, roasted potatoes and crispy onion rings. Vegan on request.

04 Dessert Same in both packages

Ice cream cake — layered vanilla, chocolate and pistachio finished with a warm syrup drizzle. Served as the night turns to the show's final act, before coffee and tea service.

Unlimited soft drinks (water, cola, juice) included in both packages. Unlimited local alcohol available as a +€15 add-on; imported spirits at extra cost. Dietary needs (vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free) handled on board — let us know on booking.

Turkish Night Show · Live on Stage

Shows every night

A four-act Turkish night programme, performed live every evening between dinner and the DJ — concert-grade lighting, professional performers, no interval.

The programme · hour by hour

Four acts, back to back

Performances run from roughly 21:30 to 22:30, while dinner continues at your table. Each act flows into the next — from the serenity of the opening dervish to the energetic peak of the belly dance.

01 Whirling Dervish Opens the night · ~21:30

The serene opening of the evening — a Mevlevi sema. The dervish spins in white robes around a central axis, one hand turned to the sky, one to the earth. Originating in 13th-century Konya with the poet Rumi, the dance is both spiritual ritual and theatrical art. A slow, contemplative start before the night builds in energy.

02 Anatolian Folk Dance ~21:50

A folk dance company in regional costumes performs across Anatolia's cultural map — Black Sea horon, Aegean zeybek, eastern halay. Energetic, percussive, accompanied by traditional Anatolian instruments. The cultural breadth of Turkey in fifteen minutes.

03 Drum Ensemble ~22:10

A professional percussion ensemble takes the deck — davul, darbuka and bendir layered over each other in driving rhythms. The room's energy lifts; this is where the night turns from observation to participation, guests clapping along, lights washing across the deck.

04 Belly Dance The night's peak · ~22:30

The evening's centrepiece — a professional belly dancer's full routine, weaving between tables and the stage. Bright sequined costumes, expressive choreography, audience moments with guests pulled gently into the dance. The kind of moment guests photograph and remember for years.

After the four acts, the DJ takes the deck until midnight (see Party). All performers are professionally trained, with full-time stage and music careers — concert-grade lighting and sound across the main deck.

View · Between Two Continents

View after dark

A three-hour cruise across Europe and Asia — bridges, palaces and the illuminated silhouette of Istanbul.

The Three-Hour Route
Eminönü
Departure
Heart of the historic peninsula, where the Bosphorus meets the Golden Horn. Our evening begins here, at our pier with Galata Tower across the water.
Karaköy
European
Once Galata's harbour, today a vibrant district of European-era architecture, art galleries and waterfront cafés.
Dolmabahçe Palace
European
Sultan Abdülmecid's grand baroque palace, opened in 1856 — the seat of Ottoman power for the empire's final decades.
Çırağan Palace
European
The last imperial palace, built for Sultan Abdülaziz in 1871. Today, a luxury hotel rising directly from the strait.
Ortaköy Mosque
European
An iconic baroque-revival mosque (1856) whose marble façade is mirrored in the Bosphorus — postcard of Istanbul.
Bosphorus Bridge
Bridge
The first bridge to span the strait, opened in 1973. Two continents joined overhead — illuminated nightly in changing colours.
Bebek
European
A storied European bay, famed for its waterfront mansions, cafés and elegant promenade — Istanbul's calm corner.
Rumeli Fortress
European
Built in just four months in 1452 by Mehmed II — the fortress that sealed Constantinople's fate before the conquest.
FSM Bridge
Mid-point
The strait's second bridge (1988), named for Mehmed the Conqueror. Mid-point of the cruise — we turn here for the Asian shore.
Beylerbeyi Palace
Asian
The summer palace of the Ottoman sultans (1865), where royal guests were received on the Asian shore.
Üsküdar
Asian
The Asian heart of Istanbul — ancient Chrysopolis. Mihrimah Sultan Mosque, narrow streets and time-worn quaysides.
Maiden's Tower
From afar
A legendary 18th-century tower at the heart of the strait. We pass close enough to see it lit, far enough to keep its mystery.
Eminönü
Arrival
Back to where the evening began — the pier, the city, the night closing in. Three hours, two continents, one Bosphorus.

On the European side: Dolmabahçe Palace, Çırağan, Ortaköy Mosque, the 15 July Martyrs Bridge in red light. On the Asian side: Beylerbeyi Palace, Kuzguncuk houses, Anadolu Hisarı. At the midpoint, the Fatih Sultan Mehmet Bridge; on the return, Maiden's Tower and the Topkapı silhouette.

Party · DJ & Dance Floor

Party under the stars

After the Turkish night show, the party shifts to the sky deck — open-air, a resident DJ, dancing under the Bosphorus stars until midnight.

After the show

A real party, without the show-off

No celebrity DJ acts and no laser theatre — just an open-air dance floor on the sky deck, a resident DJ who reads the crowd, and a Bosphorus skyline as backdrop. Dance if you like; the lounge deck stays calm if you don't.

01 The shift ~22:30 · After the belly dance

As the four-act Turkish night show closes with the belly dance, the lights change and the DJ takes the deck. Dinner service quietens; coffee and drinks continue. Anyone ready to dance moves up to the sky deck — those who prefer to linger over a drink stay at their table.

02 The sky deck Open-air · second bar

The top of the boat opens to the sky — dance floor in the centre, lounge seating around the edge, the second bar serving cocktails and the Bosphorus on every side. In warmer months the breeze does the cooling; in winter the deck is heated.

03 The DJ Resident · reads the crowd

A resident DJ — professional, well practised on this floor, with full sound and lighting at hand. The set leans into whatever the room wants on the night: a young couple's evening reads different to a friends-of-six birthday. No name on a marquee; we'd rather over-deliver than over-promise.

04 Theme by night Loose, never strict

A rough weekly cadence we keep to — but the DJ always adapts to the crowd:

  • Monday · Latin warm-up
  • Tuesday · Deep house
  • Wednesday · 90s & vintage hits
  • Thursday · R&B
  • Friday · Commercial hits
  • Saturday · Open format · the crowd decides
  • Sunday · Lounge — slower, jazzier

Private charters available for birthdays, bachelor / bachelorette parties and corporate events — message us on WhatsApp or Telegram for a custom evening. The DJ booth can host a guest DJ if you bring one.

The Programme · Hour by Hour

An evening, step by step

From a 21:00 departure at Eminönü Pier to a midnight return — the full Bosphorus arc.

Begin

Welcome

20:30

Doors open at 20:00. Our pier at Eminönü looks out from the historic peninsula onto one of the Bosphorus's most beautiful vistas — framed by the silhouette of Galata Tower. If time allows, watching the sunset from the boat's deck is a pleasure of its own. Once aboard, your reservation is confirmed and, at your private table, the evening opens with our exquisite meze service.

Dinner

21:00

While you enjoy a variety of seasonal mezes and drinks, your main course is served alongside a seasonal salad. On one side, the Bosphorus's unmatched view; on the other, the stage programme — savour your meal, take in the show, drift into the view; the evening unfolds at the tempo you choose.

The Show

21:30

The stage opens with the serenity of a whirling dervish — Anatolian folk dances and a drum ensemble lift the night's energy, climaxing in belly dance. As your dinner continues, a four-act Anatolian evening unfolds before you, all from your table.

Finale

DJ Party

22:30

After the show, momentum carries the night forward — the DJ takes over and the dance floor opens. For those seeking calm, the upper deck offers its own pleasure: sip your drink and take in the Bosphorus's colourful panorama; we recommend plenty of photographs. Dance floor or upper deck — until midnight, the choice is yours.

Frequently Asked
What tours do you offer?
An evening Bosphorus cruise — we depart at 21:00 and return around midnight. On our three-deck vessel: full dinner, a Turkish night programme (whirling dervish, belly dance, folk, drums), DJ, and unlimited soft drinks. Departing from our pier at Eminönü.
Are alcoholic drinks included?
Soft drinks are included in all packages. For alcohol: an unlimited local package per person at +€15 (beer, rakı, gin, vodka, red & white wine). Imported drinks (champagne, whisky, cognac, etc.) are served at extra cost.
Where does the cruise depart from and return to?
We depart from our pier at Eminönü at 21:00 and return to the same point around midnight. Doors open at 20:00 — feel free to come earlier and enjoy the sunset from the pier.
What route does the cruise follow?
Eminönü → Karaköy → Dolmabahçe → Çırağan → Ortaköy Mosque → Bosphorus Bridge → Bebek → Rumeli Fortress → FSM Bridge → Beylerbeyi → Üsküdar → Maiden's Tower (from afar) → Eminönü. The route may vary depending on weather and sea conditions.
Are tables close to the stage?
The panoramic windows of our salon offer clear Bosphorus views from every seat. The stage is positioned to be visible from all tables. VIP package guests are seated closest to the stage.
What shows and entertainment are there?
A live programme runs throughout the evening: whirling dervishes (a serene opening), Anatolian folk dances, drum ensemble, belly dance (the night's peak), and after dinner, a DJ performance. Concert-grade sound and lighting.
Can I book for the same day?
Yes — same-day bookings are possible up to 1 hour before departure (20:00). Message us on WhatsApp or Telegram and we'll check availability instantly.
How do I pay?
No prepayment is required. All payment is made on board. We accept cash (EUR, TRY, USD) and card.
Is hotel transfer available?
Yes — hotel-to-pier-to-hotel transfer is available at +€5 per person. Before the tour, our driver collects you from your hotel and brings you to the pier; after the tour, the same driver waits at the pier and drops you back at your hotel. No need to plan transportation.
Can I cancel or change my reservation?
Free cancellation is possible up to 2 hours before departure (until 19:00). Since there's no prepayment, the process is hassle-free — just send us a message.
What happens in bad weather?
The vessel has a covered salon, semi-open and open decks. In rain, the open deck closes while the salon and stage continue uninterrupted. The cruise runs regardless of weather conditions.
Is it suitable for all ages?
Yes, the cruise is open to guests of all ages. Please contact our reservations team for child pricing and child-friendly menu options.
Can you arrange surprises for special occasions?
Yes — for anniversaries, birthdays, marriage proposals and other special moments, we offer romantic-table, cake & champagne service and professional photography add-ons. You can add them from the Extras section or mention them at reservation.

Privacy · Data Protection

Your privacy, respected

How we collect, use, and protect your personal data — short, clear, no jargon.

Who we are

Luxury Bosphorus Tour ("we", "us") operates this site and the booking conversations on WhatsApp and Telegram. For any privacy-related question, message us through either channel.

What we collect

When you reserve, we collect only what we need to confirm and operate your evening:

  • Your name and phone number
  • The date, package, party size, and any add-ons you've chosen
  • Your hotel name / pickup address and optional room number — only when you request hotel transfer
  • Any free-text messages you send us

We do not ask for ID numbers, credit-card data, or any document. Payment is taken on board.

Why we collect it

  • To confirm your reservation and hold your table
  • To coordinate hotel pickup, when applicable
  • To reach you with last-minute updates (weather, time changes)
  • To honour cancellation requests

Who we share it with

Only with the staff who need it to deliver your evening:

  • Our transfer driver, if you've chosen hotel pickup — receives only your hotel / room and name
  • Our reception team at the pier, to recognise your reservation

We do not sell, rent, or trade your data. We do not pass anything to advertisers.

Third-party services used on this site

  • Google Fonts — for typography (no personal data is sent)
  • OpenStreetMap — only when you search for your hotel; the search query is sent to OSM's Nominatim service to return results
  • WhatsApp / Telegram — when you tap "Confirm via …" you continue the booking in their respective apps; their own privacy policies then apply

How long we keep it

Active booking conversations are kept while customer support remains useful — typically up to 12 months after your evening. After that, personal details are deleted.

Your rights

You can ask us to see, correct, or delete any data we hold about you. Message us on WhatsApp or Telegram and we'll process the request promptly.

Cookies

This site does not set tracking cookies. Your browser may locally cache fonts and map tiles for performance.

Terms · Booking Conditions

Booking terms

The basic rules of the evening — what we promise, what we ask in return.

1. Reservation

A reservation is held when we confirm it on WhatsApp or Telegram and send you a confirmation message. We accept guests up to one hour before departure, subject to availability. No prepayment is required.

2. Departure and duration

Departure is at 21:00 from our pier at Eminönü. Doors open at 20:00 — please arrive in good time. The cruise runs for three hours and returns to the same pier around midnight.

3. Pricing and payment

Prices shown on this site are valid at booking. Adults pay full price, kids aged 4–7 pay half, babies aged 0–3 are free. Add-ons (transfer, alcohol, romantic table) are calculated per booking. Payment is taken on board in cash (EUR, TRY, USD) or by card.

4. Hotel transfer

If you choose pickup, our driver collects you at the hotel time we agree on WhatsApp. We need your hotel name and a confirmed pickup window. The same driver drops you back after the cruise.

5. Cancellation and changes

Free cancellation is available up to 2 hours before departure. See our Cancellation policy for details.

6. Weather and sea conditions

The cruise runs in almost all weather. In rare storm conditions the route may be shortened or the evening rescheduled — you are entitled to a free reschedule or to walk away with no charge.

7. Behaviour on board

We welcome a lively evening, but we reserve the right to refuse boarding to anyone who is significantly intoxicated, behaves aggressively, or endangers other guests or crew. The captain's authority is final at sea.

8. Liability

Operations are governed by Turkish maritime and consumer law. We carry full passenger insurance for the cruise.

9. Photography and video

You may photograph and film for personal use. If we want to publish any image that identifies guests, we will ask first.

10. Updates to these terms

We may update these terms from time to time. The version published on this page at the time of your booking is the one that applies to you.

Cancellation · Refunds

Cancellation policy

How free cancellation works and what happens in special cases.

Free cancellation

Cancel for any reason — no questions, no fee — up to 2 hours before departure (i.e. by 19:00 on the evening of your cruise). Since we don't take prepayment, there's nothing to refund and nothing to chase. Just message us.

How to cancel

Reply to the WhatsApp or Telegram thread you used to book, or start a new message — name and date are enough for us to find your reservation. Confirmation arrives within minutes during operating hours.

Inside the 2-hour window or no-show

If you cancel after 19:00 or simply don't arrive, we don't chase a charge — but the table is held until departure and another guest cannot use it. Please send us a quick note when you can; it helps the table go to someone else.

Weather or sea conditions

If we cancel due to weather, captain's call, or vessel availability, we offer you either a free reschedule for any future evening within 6 months, or a full release if you've already paid anything in advance.

Changes (date, party size, package)

You can change your date, guest count, or package up to 4 hours before departure, subject to availability. Date changes are free; upgrades pay the difference; downgrades follow the current published rates.

Refunds, if applicable

Most evenings carry no prepayment, so refunds rarely apply. In the few cases where a deposit was taken (private yacht, large groups), refunds are made through the same channel as the payment within 5 working days of the cancellation request.
